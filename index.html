About Alex

Building for the
people overlooked.

Alex is an innovator building technology for the communities emerging tech tends to leave behind.

Alex runs Braveon AI — a multi-venture studio incubating tools that solve real problems in underserved communities.

The portfolio spans climate tech (CarbonSight, an offline-first carbon footprint tracker with 1,000+ users, presented at AWS re:Invent 2025 and the World Bank Summit 2026), civic tech, AI safety research and education hardware.

Alex has also served and mentored 200+ young people in his local community on technology and entrepreneurship.

Alex is a Community Builder, Google Cloud Innovator, and Tableau Ambassador, and a Silver Award winner of the Queen's Commonwealth Essay Competition 2025.

Alex's mission: ensure builders from underserved communities aren't shut out of the technologies shaping the future and give them the tools to shape it themselves.

FAQ / Common questions
What does my brand receive?

Your approved logo is printed on the selected polo placement, worn by Alex at GitHub Universe 2026, and included in the campaign record.

How many brands can participate?

There are 20 placements. The premium back statement is reserved for one company or brand at $10,000.

What file should I upload?

Upload a clear PNG, JPEG, or SVG logo. We will confirm production sizing and placement before printing.

How does bidding work?

Most front placements start at $500. Left and right shoulders start at $1,000, and the center chest starts at $2,000. Select a row, upload your mark, and submit your bid.

When is payment taken?

After placement approval, you will receive UPI or bank-transfer instructions. Payment is made manually in INR; there is no card checkout.

Can I change or remove my logo?

Requests are reviewed before production. Changes after print approval may not be possible and may incur replacement costs.
